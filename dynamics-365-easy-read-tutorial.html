What the marks mean

Click me

An orange ring means: click this.

An arrow means: now do the next one.

The pictures are drawings, not photos. Your screen may look a bit different.

Step 1 of 3

Order it

Six clicks. Follow the arrows down the page.

1Open the order list.
Modules Procurement and sourcing Purchase orders All purchase orders
2Click New.
+ New
3Pick who you are buying from.
Vendor accountV-1042

Type a few letters. Then pick from the list.

4Click OK.
OK
5Add a line for each thing you want.
+ Add line Item numberM0012 Quantity250

The price fills in by itself. Check it looks right.

6Save. Then confirm.
Save Purchase tab Confirm

Save first. Confirm does not work until you have saved.

Done. Now wait for the delivery. Go to step 2.

Step 2 of 3

It arrives

The boxes turn up. Write down what came.

First, find the delivery note.

It comes with the boxes. It has a number on it. You will need that number.

1Open the order.

Same list as before. Click the order number.

2Click the Receive tab.
Receive
3Click Product receipt.
Product receipt
4Type the delivery note number and the date.
Product receiptDN-77421 Product receipt date26 Feb 2026

Use the date the boxes came. Not today's date.

5Type how many came.
QuantityDeliver now Deliver now40

Count the boxes. Type the real number. Type 0 if nothing came.

6Click OK.
OK

Did it all come?

Yes, all of it.

You are done. Go to step 3.

No, some is missing.

That is fine. When the rest comes, do step 2 again with the new delivery note.

Step 3 of 3

Pay the bill

The bill comes from the seller. Check it. Then post it.

1Open the order.

Same list as before.

2Click the Invoice tab.
Invoice
3Click Invoice.
Invoice
4Type the bill number and the bill date.
NumberINV-2026-4417 Invoice date28 Feb 2026

Copy them off the bill. Do not make them up.

5Tick the deliveries this bill is for.
Match product receipts
6Look at the mark on each line.

Green tick

The numbers agree. Good.

Orange triangle

Something does not agree. Stop. Ask someone.

7All green? Click Post.
Post

If any line is orange, do not post yet.

All done. The money side is now with the finance team.

Help

If it will not work

Find your problem on the left. The fix is on the right.

Confirm is grey. I cannot click it.
Click Save first.
Still grey after saving.
Your order needs a manager to approve it. Look for a Workflow button.
It says the receipt number is already used.
Someone may have done this delivery already. Check before you type a new number.
More came than I ordered.
Do not force it. Talk to the person who made the order.
The bill will not post.
Look for an orange triangle on a line. Save the bill and pass it on.
I posted something wrong.
Do not delete it. Ask a colleague to help you fix it.
