When it will not work

The problems people actually hit

Confirm is greyed out

Save the order first. If it is still greyed out, your company requires approval — use Workflow › Submit and wait, or you may simply not have permission to confirm.

The product receipt number is rejected

It has been used on this order before. Check whether the delivery was already posted by someone else before you invent a new number.

You received more than was ordered

Most companies block this. Talk to whoever raised the order about increasing the quantity rather than forcing the receipt through.

The invoice will not post

Look at the Match column. A difference in price or quantity beyond your company's tolerance needs approval, not a correction typed over the top. Save the invoice and pass it on.

You posted something wrong

Posted documents are not deleted — they are corrected with a reversing entry. Do not try to fix it by editing or deleting. Ask Accounts payable or a colleague who has done it before.

Plain English

Words you will keep seeing

PostTo commit a document for real. Posting updates stock and accounts, and cannot be undone by deleting.
Product receiptThe record of goods arriving. Also the name of the vendor's packing slip number you type in.
Deliver nowThe quantity arriving in this particular delivery, as opposed to the quantity ordered.
Three-way matchingThe automatic check that the order, the receipt and the invoice agree with one another.
Site and WarehouseWhere goods physically live. Site is the location; warehouse is a store within it.
Legal entityThe company you are working in, shown in the top corner. Records cannot be moved between companies.
Accounts payableThe part of the system, and the team, that pays vendors.
