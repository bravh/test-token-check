Receive the goods

When the delivery arrives at your door, you record it. This is called posting a product receipt, and it is what makes the stock in Dynamics 365 match the stock on your shelf.

Have the vendor's paperwork in front of you

Every delivery comes with a packing slip or delivery note, and it carries a reference number. You will be asked to type that number in. It is how anyone looking at this order later can tie the entry in Dynamics 365 back to a piece of paper in a folder.

1

Open the confirmed order

Go back to the list of orders and click the order number to open it.

Modules › Procurement and sourcing › Purchase orders › All purchase orders

You are looking for one with the status Open order that has already been confirmed. If it is easier, filter the list by vendor.

2

Go to the Receive tab

Along the top of the order there is a row of tabs. Click Receive. The buttons underneath change. In the Generate group, click Product receipt.

3

Type the vendor's reference and the date

Two fields matter here:

  • Product receipt — copy the number from the vendor's packing slip. It is compulsory, and it must be different from any receipt number already used on this order.
  • Product receipt date — the day the goods actually arrived, which is not always today. Get this right: it decides the accounting period the stock lands in.
4

Say how much actually turned up

The Quantity box near the top decides what the lines are filled with:

  • Ordered quantity — use this when the whole delivery arrived complete. Every line is filled with the full amount.
  • Deliver now — use this when only part of it arrived. The lines are filled with whatever you type in the Deliver now column, and you type the real figures yourself.

Scroll to the Lines grid at the bottom of the window and check every row against the boxes in front of you. Two short of what was ordered? Type the number you actually got. Nothing at all on a line? Type 0 and that line stays open for a later delivery.

5

Post it

Click OK. The window closes and the order reloads. If you need a printed copy for the warehouse, tick Print product receipt before you click.

6

Check that it worked

Three things change straight away, and they are all worth a glance:

  • The order's Status becomes Received if everything came, or stays Open order with a partial amount showing if some is still outstanding.
  • The Received column on the lines now shows the amounts you entered, and Remaining drops accordingly.
  • A record of the receipt appears under Receive › Journals › Product receipt. Open it if you ever need to prove what was received, when, and against which packing slip.
The three that trip people up
  • “Product receipt number already exists.” That reference has been used on this order before. Check whether somebody already posted this delivery before you type a new number.
  • You cannot receive more than you ordered unless your company allows over-delivery. If more turned up than the order says, speak to whoever raised the order rather than forcing the number through.
  • Posting cannot be undone by deleting it. If you get a quantity wrong, the fix is to post a correcting receipt with a negative amount — ask a colleague to walk you through it the first time.
If your warehouse registers arrivals first

Some organisations scan goods in at the loading bay before anyone posts a receipt — through Arrival overview or a handheld scanner. If that is how yours works, the quantities are already recorded by the time you get here: choose Registered quantity in the Quantity box instead of typing amounts yourself, and the rest of the step is identical.
